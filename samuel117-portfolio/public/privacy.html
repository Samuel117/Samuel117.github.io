Privacy Policy — Massive Messages / Sistema de Gestión de Bienhechores

Last updated: June 2026

1. Identity and Contact Information

This privacy policy applies to the application Massive Messages, developed and operated by Samuel Padilla on behalf of Arquidiócesis de Guadalajara Seminario Diocesano (the "Seminary"). For privacy-related inquiries: samuel.padilla45@gmail.com

To report security vulnerabilities in this application, contact: samuel.padilla45@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

This application processes the following data:

Data obtained from Meta's Platform:

  • WhatsApp Business API access tokens
  • WhatsApp Business Account identifiers
  • Phone Number IDs associated with the Seminary's WhatsApp Business Account
  • Message delivery status and metadata provided by Meta's API

Data managed internally on behalf of the Seminary:

Donor data:

  • Full name of the account holder (contractHolder, accountHolderName)
  • WhatsApp number, mobile phone number, and home phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Date of birth
  • Client number and internal reference
  • Donor type (Prospect / Donor) and payment modality (direct debit, home collection, deposit/transfer)
  • Banking information: bank name, account type (credit card / debit card / CLABE), and account number
  • Active/inactive status and deactivation date

Payment data:

  • Expected amount and amount paid per period
  • Payment date and status (paid / unpaid)
  • Transaction reference code

Message history:

  • Recipient donor name
  • Message type (first contact, payment reminder, thank you, birthday, campaign, direct send)
  • Meta message category (MARKETING / UTILITY)
  • Send date and time
  • Delivery result (successful / failed)
  • Number of messages sent per operation

Message configuration:

  • Configured WhatsApp templates (name, ID, language, Meta category)
  • Dynamic variables per template

3. How We Process Data

Data is processed through the following operations: collection, storage, use, and transmission via Meta's WhatsApp Business API. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties outside of what is strictly necessary to operate the WhatsApp Business API.

The only external service used is the WhatsApp Business API (Meta), subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy.

4. Purpose of Processing

Data is processed exclusively for the following permitted purposes:

  • Internal donor management for the Seminary
  • Sending WhatsApp communications via Meta-approved message templates (payment reminders, confirmations, welcome messages, announcements)
  • Tracking donation history on behalf of the Seminary

Data obtained from Meta's Platform is not used for any purpose beyond what is described in this policy and permitted by Meta's Developer Policies.

5. Processing on Behalf of a Client (Tech Provider)

Samuel Padilla acts as a technology provider for the Seminary. All Platform Data obtained from Meta is processed exclusively on behalf of and under the direction of the Seminary, solely to help them use the platform. This data is not used for independent commercial purposes by the developer.

Data belonging to the Seminary is maintained separately and is not mixed with data from any other client.

The developer will notify the Seminary of any communication received from Meta regarding user data requests.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it is necessary for a legitimate business purpose as directed by the Seminary, or until the Seminary or the data subject requests its deletion. Upon request, data will be permanently deleted within a reasonable timeframe.
